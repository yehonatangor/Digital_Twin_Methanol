## Numerical parameters taken from the literature

Every correlation constant in this repository is a value published by someone else, and each one carries its citation at the point of use. Individual facts and measured constants are not themselves copyrightable, but the papers and books they appear in are, so the constants are used here as cited data and the sources are named in `docs/24-validation.md` and `docs/25-model-provenance.md`.

The principal sources are Van-Dal and Bouallou (2013), Mucci et al. (2023), Lim et al. (2022), Shi et al. (2020), Xu and Froment (1989), Arab Aboosadi et al. (2011), Fichtl et al., Perry's Chemical Engineers' Handbook 8th edition, and Turton et al., *Analysis, Synthesis and Design of Chemical Processes*, 5th edition.

## ChemSep databank

Peng-Robinson binary interaction parameters and NRTL binary parameters are taken from the ChemSep databank, which is distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License. Only parameter values are used; no ChemSep source code is included, linked or derived from. The databank remains under its own terms and its authors retain their rights in it.

## Turton Appendix A tables

The CAPCOST equipment-cost constants of Tables A.1, A.2, A.4, A.5 and the flooding constants of Table 21.7 are transcribed from the 5th edition. The tables belong to their publisher; they are reproduced here only as the numerical inputs the cost model needs, and the book is required to use them in any other way.

## No proprietary process data

Nothing in this repository comes from a commercial plant, a vendor quotation or a licensor's process package. Every operating point is either a published figure or an output of this model, and the ones that are this model's own output state that.
